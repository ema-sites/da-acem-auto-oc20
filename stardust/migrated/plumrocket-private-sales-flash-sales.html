Overview

Back to top

Private Sales & Flash Sales module allows merchants to schedule Private Sale events on their store with member-only access. They can also run Flash Sales for a limited time. These types of promotion significantly increase your store’s conversion rate and account registrations. Moreover, Flash Sale events allow you to sell a large number of items in a short time, thus liquidating excess inventory.


Features:

  • Set an event countdown timer and give your customers an impression of urgency. Hence, prompt them to get a bargain before the time ends.
  • Maximize your sales and account registrations by running private events for members only. They generate excitement around private events, so clients are more willing to participate.
  • Organize your events on Homepages and inform customers about current, upcoming, and ending soon events in one place. 


Offer to Join a Private Sale & Create Exclusivity

Take advantage of running private events in different ways:

  1. Members Only Shopping: Transform your Magento store to a shopping club or create a membership website that requires users to sign up to access exclusive offers. A custom-looking Splash Page will help you hide special deals from public view and invite potential buyers to join with a sign-in or registration form.
  2. Private Sale Events: Convert any categories or single products into Private Sale events for registered customers only. At the same time, allow all visitors to browse other categories and make purchases.
  3. Catalog Event Widgets: Use Event Widgets to display and promote Private & Flash Sale events on a category page, homepage, and other pages on your Magento store.   


Configure Different Access Restrictions for Private Events

When creating Private Events, you can configure default access permissions depending on a customer group and event status, or set them for each event individually:

  • Restrict selected customer groups from browsing events, viewing prices, and making purchases.
  • Configure different access permissions for ended, active, and future events.


Schedule Limited-Time Flash Sales

Convert categories and single products to Magento Flash Sale events on your store. Schedule a flash sale and set a countdown timer for website products that automatically starts displaying the remaining time and disappears when the time is up. A countdown entices buyers to buy now and not to regret a missed deal later, which leads to a significant increase in revenue in a matter of days or even hours.

When a sale is over, you have several options on how to display products: 

  • Keep selling them at full price.
  • Hide Add To Cart button, so customers cannot make a purchase. At the same time, allow them to browse products with prices hidden or displayed.
  • Hide products from or all visitors or some customer groups.


Attract Customers With Daily Deals & Sell More

Create a Daily Deals category and set a countdown timer for each product separately. Similar to Flash Sales Events, Daily Deals help generate quick revenue and provide a positive shopping experience to your clients. 


Schedule Future Sales on Events Timeline

Use a visual event timeline and run your marketing campaign more effectively, as it displays all ended, active, and upcoming events in one place. Furthermore, an event grid allows you to filter events by their status, type, sales date, and more to build an overall picture. 


Set Event Discounts & Boost Sales

The extension provides you with a handy event management interface, allowing you to set product discounts with lightning speed. Use mass actions and update a discount amount, flash sale price, and quantity limit, or change them for each product individually. What is more, there is no need to monitor the timing and cancel discounts manually, as they will be automatically disabled when an event ends. 


Use Event Statistics & Measure Success

Be always aware of events’ progress by monitoring their real-time statistics. Learn and analyze the total revenue, the number of placed orders, and new registrations within each event. Measuring success helps know your clients’ preferences and plan future events more effectively. 


Preview Future Events on any Date

We have provided you with an event preview mode and made it easy to plan future Private and Flash Sale Events. Make sure your event will look the way you expect by previewing it live. 


Attract Visitors With Stunning Visuals

Choose a video over a picture for an event thumbnail to make events more aesthetically appealing, as well as more likely to be clicked and viewed. The extension allows you to automatically play the thumbnail video when moving a cursor over it.

Likewise, use a Splash Page Video to make an instant wow effect as soon as a customer comes to your website. Besides, you can configure a slideshow or a single image background for your Splash Page and enable it on special events and dates. 


Use Homepages to Organize Events

Make your Magento store well-organized and easy to navigate with event Homepages. Take advantage of new display types of categories in Magento 2 frontend that contain Flash Sales templates. Create one Flash Sale Homepage and group your events in one place. Otherwise, use several homepages (e.g., Men, Women, Gifts) to display events on separate relevant pages. 


Sell More with Countdown Timers

Place multiple timers throughout your Magento store and drive customers to stop delaying and start buying: 

  • Use a total of 5 custom-looking header templates with countdown timers on category pages.
  • Count down the sale on every product to remind visitors of a limited time to get a deal.


Generate Newsletter Event Templates in Few Clicks

Save time informing your clients about current or coming soon events: create your custom template and generate newsletters in no time. The extension also ensures the smooth operation of newsletter templates with any email marketing service. 


Enjoy Compatibility with Custom & Third-Party Magento Themes

The Private Sales & Flash Sales Extension for Magento 2 ensures seamless work on any custom and third-party Magento themes. Moreover, use the extension on Magento 2 Community and Enterprise editions and increase your store’s revenue.
