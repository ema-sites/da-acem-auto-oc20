Overview

Back to top

 

Prepare your e-commerce business to comply with the new pricing requirements of the EU Omnibus Directive, which is already in effect and applies to all retailers who sell products and services to EU customers. Keep the price change policy transparent and consistent to build customer loyalty and avoid unforeseen fines. 

The Omnibus Price Tracker for Magento 2 extension captures price changes made in your store, including catalog price rules and special prices. Using this data, the extension automatically calculates and displays the lowest price from a 30-day (or longer) period before a price reduction, as required by the Omnibus Directive. 

 

Key Features:

  • Indicate the minimum product price in the specified period prior to price reduction; 
  • Place historical prices both on product and category pages; 
  • Display Omnibus Prices for all or only for discounted products;   
  • Monitor all price changes in one place;
  • Configure the automatic clearing for the price change log;
  • Individually capture price changes and showcase the lowest prices for every configured website;
  • Hyva-ready storefront. 

 

30-Day Lowest Price (Omnibis Price) 

The extension’s configuration allows you to set any duration in days as a basis for calculating the lowest product price. But according to the Omnibus Directive, this duration must not be shorter than 30 days prior to the application of price reduction. 

 

Placement Of The Block With Omnibus Price

It is possible to show the Omnibus Price block on product and category pages for all or only for discounted products. 

 

Price Change History Grid

View and manage all price changes that are made in the store after the extension installation on a handy grid. Use various filters and their combinations to easily find the recordings you need. You can filter price change recordings by ID, SKU, date, customer group, and new or latest price.    

 

Automatic Price Change Log Clearing

Regularly remove old price change recordings from the log. Protect your database from overloads by configuring automatic price change log clearing or removing unnecessary recordings manually with the help of the ‘delete’ mass action.
